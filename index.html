Help & FAQ

Everything you need to know about using the ASTU ECE Portal.

Getting Started
This portal is your one-stop hub for ASTU ECE course materials — notes, assignments, mid & final exams, and tutorial videos for Year 2 Sem 2 and Year 3. Open the Menu button at the top right to see all the tools. Tap any card (Find Materials, Video Tutorials, GPA Calculator, Minesweeper, etc.) to jump into that section. Use the Menu button or your phone's back gesture to return to the home screen at any time.
Finding Files
Open Find Materials from the Menu. Use the search bar to look for any file name, course code, or course title. Tap the Filter accordion to narrow results by Year → Semester → Course → Type (Note, Assignment, Mid, Final, Test). Results appear as folders — tap a folder to see the files inside, then tap Open to download. To go back to the folder list, tap Back to Folders. You can also sort by Newest First or Oldest First using the dropdown. If you get stuck on "No materials found," just tap 🗑 Clear Filters to reset everything.
Favorites (Stars)
Tap the star icon on any folder, file, or video to save it. Favorites are stored on your Telegram account, so they follow you across every device you log into — phone, tablet, desktop. To see everything you've saved, open Saved Favorites from the Menu. From there you can search, browse saved folders, and re-open files with one tap. Tap the star again to remove a favorite.
Notifications & Updates
The bell icon at the top shows all official announcements from the admins — old ones stay there forever, and a red badge tells you how many are unread. Tap any announcement to open the full post on Telegram.

To get a direct message whenever new files or videos are added to a course, open that course's filter and tap Notify Me of New Files. You'll get a private DM from the bot the moment something new appears — no need to check the app manually.
3D Minesweeper
Open Minesweeper from the Menu. Pick your difficulty: Easy (8×8, 10 mines), Medium (16×16, 40 mines), or Hard (30×16, 99 mines).

Your first tap is always safe — the game places mines only after you've chosen your starting tile, and it never puts a mine on that tile or its 8 neighbors. Reveal a numbered tile and it tells you how many mines are touching it. Reveal a blank tile and the whole empty area opens up automatically. Clear every safe tile to win.

To flag a mine:
• On PC: right-click any tile.
• On mobile: tap the Flag Mode button in the toolbar (it lights up yellow), then tap tiles to place flags. Tap the button again to return to normal reveal mode. Or just long-press any tile.

Tap the Restart button any time to start a fresh board.
Uploading & Contributing
Have good notes, past papers, or a helpful YouTube lecture? We'd love to add them!

From the app: open Upload Files from the Menu, pick the Year → Semester → Course → Type, then select your files (you can select multiple at once). Tap Upload to Admin — our team reviews every submission before it goes live.

From Telegram chat: open the bot, tap Upload Material, and follow the same steps. You can send files one by one or as a batch.

Suggest a video: use the Suggest a Tutorial Link card on the home screen, paste the YouTube URL, and submit it for review.
Troubleshooting
A file won't open? Make sure you have a stable internet connection and enough storage on your phone. Some files (especially large PDFs) may take a few seconds to load from Telegram.

Found a broken or wrong file? Tap the red ⚠️ warning icon next to that item, type a short note about the problem, and send it. The admins get your report instantly and can fix or replace the file. Every report helps the whole community.

Something else not working? Open Rate & Feedback from the Menu and tell us what happened — we read every message.
