Help & FAQ

How do I get notifications?
Go to Find Materials or Video Tutorials, select your course, and click the "Notify Me" button. You'll get a direct message when new files are added!
I found a broken file. What do I do?
Click the red ⚠️ icon next to any material or video. It will automatically send a report with your comment to the admins so they can fix it.
Is my GPA data saved online?
No! The GPA calculator runs entirely on your phone. We do not save or monitor your grades.
